# Recall Atlas — Brand Identity

## Approved name

**Recall Atlas**

Use two words in the primary wordmark. Do not use a hyphen in the normal display name unless a technical identifier requires it.

## Approved tagline

**Find it. Understand it. Remember it.**

This is the chosen tagline. Preserve the punctuation and order unless a future brand review deliberately changes it.

## Brand idea

Recall Atlas connects two complementary ideas:

- **Recall:** retrieving knowledge, revisiting previous questions, and practicing what has been learned.
- **Atlas:** an organized map through a large collection of academic information.

Together, the name suggests a navigable academic knowledge collection rather than a single-purpose exam-upload site. The brand can extend from past questions into notes, articles, learning resources, and academic announcements without losing coherence.

## Positioning statement

Recall Atlas is a searchable academic knowledge platform that helps students discover past questions and learning resources, understand their context, and return to them when studying.

## Brand promise

Make useful academic material easier to find, trace, organize, and revisit.

## Personality

- Clear and dependable rather than flashy.
- Academic but approachable.
- Technically capable without making AI the whole identity.
- Community-oriented while taking quality and safety seriously.
- Useful to students and educators across multiple schools.

## Messaging pillars

### Find it
Unified search across questions, source documents, and other resources; filters for grade, subject, topic, year, curriculum, and source; keyword search with semantic retrieval when available.

### Understand it
Show original source context, question text, diagrams, document page references, and relevant related material. Do not imply the platform provides answers in the initial release.

### Remember it
Support bookmarks, personal collections, recently viewed items, study-later lists, and later study-progress features.

## Suggested short description

“Recall Atlas is a searchable academic archive where students can discover past questions, explore their original sources, and organize trusted learning resources.”

## Suggested longer description

“Recall Atlas helps school communities preserve and discover academic knowledge. Upload past tests and learning materials, extract questions with links to their original documents, search by subject and topic, and build a personal study collection. AI-assisted processing reduces manual organization, while source provenance and quality controls help keep the archive reliable.”

## Visual direction

The name supports a visual system inspired by maps, indexes, linked nodes, coordinates, pathways, and structured knowledge. Avoid making the interface look like a literal navigation app; the map metaphor should support the academic product, not overwhelm it.

Potential visual elements:
- A modular grid or atlas/index motif.
- Connected points representing questions, topics, and source documents.
- Clear typography and strong hierarchy.
- Restrained use of color to communicate status, not decoration.
- A distinct icon that remains legible at favicon and mobile-app sizes.

## Tone of voice

Use concise, specific language. Explain AI-assisted actions in plain terms. Avoid claims such as “perfect extraction,” “guaranteed accurate,” or “all answers solved.” Prefer “extracted from the source,” “suggested metadata,” “needs review,” and “source page.”
